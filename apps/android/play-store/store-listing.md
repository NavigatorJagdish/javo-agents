## Full Description
Javo Agents is the mobile companion for your local-first Javo Agents Windows workspace. Manage your 29-agent team, review Supervised Autopilot approvals, draft content offline, and issue English, Hindi, or Hinglish voice commands from anywhere.

### Key Features
- **Supervised Autopilot Approvals**: Every sensitive publish or reply action waits in your Approval Inbox — Approve, Edit, Reschedule, or Reject with one tap.
- **29 Specialist Agents**: Inspect and toggle Growth Scientist, Content Factory, Brand Manager, Approval Manager, Budget Guardian, Security Guardian, and more.
- **Multilingual Voice Mode**: Push-to-talk command hub supporting English, Hindi, and Hinglish with real-time waveform state feedback (`Idle`, `Listening`, `Thinking`, `Executing`, `Finished`).
- **Offline Content Studio**: Write captions, hooks, and schedules offline in an on-device Room database; WorkManager automatically syncs with your paired Windows PC when connected and battery allows.
- **Hardware-Backed Security**: Pairing tokens and optional Gemini API keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM inside Android Keystore.
- **Instant Emergency Stop**: Pause local tasks and send an immediate halt signal to your paired Windows Local Runtime.

### Honest Availability & Pricing
- **Free ($0/month)** local-first companion app.
- Social publishing connectors (Instagram Professional, Facebook Pages, YouTube Data API) execute through your paired Windows Local Runtime with explicit human approval.
- No ad SDKs, no hidden paywalls, and no unverified background code execution.
